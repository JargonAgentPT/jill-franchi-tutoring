About Jill

A patient, encouraging approach to 11+ success

Jill Franchi (formerly known as Mrs Jarman in the classroom) has been tutoring the 11+ exam for over 25 years — including a decade teaching at grammar schools across Essex County Council. She is passionate about helping children build confidence and master the skills they need to excel.

Her approach is patient, encouraging, and tailored to each child's unique learning style — ensuring every pupil feels supported on their journey to exam day.

Jill is married to renowned songwriter Tony Franchi.
